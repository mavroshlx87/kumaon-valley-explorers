# SEO Action Plan for Kumaon Valley Explorers

Maximize search visibility and rank higher on Google using this 5-step action plan:

### 1. Optimize Local SEO (Google Business Profile)
Claim and verify your **Google Business Profile** using your physical address. Complete all fields, add high-quality photos of Kumaon destinations, and gather positive customer reviews to rank in the local map pack at the top of search pages.

### 2. Submit Sitemap to Search Console
Verify your website ownership (`https://kumaonvalley.in`) in **Google Search Console**. Submit the sitemap at `/sitemap.xml` to force Google to crawl and index all 11 routes immediately, while monitoring any coverage errors.

### 3. Target Long-Tail, Niche Keywords
Optimize package and destination content for high-intent, lower-competition search queries rather than broad terms. Focus on keywords like:
- *"Senior-friendly tour packages Kumaon"*
- *"Spiritual tour packages Om Parvat"*
- *"Taxi services Dehradun to Nainital"*

### 4. Build Authority (Content & Backlinks)
Publish helpful travel guides (e.g., preparation guides for the Om Parvat Yatra). Partner with travel bloggers and list your agency in local directories and travel forums (TripAdvisor, IndiaMike) to gain high-quality backlinks.

### 5. Leverage Speed Advantage
Maintain the website’s speed performance. The WebP conversion reduced the image payload size from **30.5MB to 4.6MB** (~85% savings) and the preloaded Hero image provides a faster Core Web Vitals speed score. Google ranks faster pages higher.
